# Should the West Little Rock Farmers Market go year-round?

*Research brief for WLRFM vendors and the advisory board — draft for review*

*Prepared August 2026*

## Why this brief exists

Several WLRFM vendors have asked about continuing the market past October 31
and making it a year-round operation. This brief pulls together what we can
document about (1) how other Arkansas markets run their seasons, (2) how the
country's most popular markets — including year-round ones in climates like
ours — structure their calendars, (3) the best available numbers on customers
and sales, and (4) a starting pros-and-cons list for the group to react to.

*(Content in progress — season tables, attendance/sales data, and the
pros/cons list are being compiled.)*
