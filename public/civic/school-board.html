School Board

First Thursdays (workshop) & third Thursdays (regular) · 5:30 PM

The NLRSD Board of Education sets the budget, policy, and leadership for North Little Rock's public schools. Workshops on the first Thursday of the month, regular voting meetings on the third — both open to the public.

Meeting facts

WhenWorkshops first Thursdays, regular meetings third Thursdays, 5:30 PM (dates can move for conflicts or holidays).

WhereNLRSD Administration Building, 2400 Willow Street, North Little Rock.

AgendasAgendas, packets, and minutes post on BoardDocs.

WatchMeetings are posted to the district's NLRSD Board Meetings YouTube channel.

Schedules can shift for holidays and special sessions — confirm against the official links below before you go.
