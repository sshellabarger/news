City Council

Second & fourth Mondays · 6:00 PM · City Hall

The council is where North Little Rock's money moves: budgets, ordinances, zoning, city contracts, and the occasional 20-year baseball lease. Eight council members — two per ward — plus the mayor, meeting twice a month in a 1914 City Hall that's on the National Register of Historic Places.

Meeting facts

WhenSecond and fourth Mondays, 6:00 PM (moves to the following Tuesday when Monday is a holiday).

WhereCity Council Chambers, City Hall, 300 Main Street, North Little Rock, AR 72114.

AgendasPosted with each meeting on the city's meeting calendar.

WatchThe city has streamed council meetings on its Facebook page.
