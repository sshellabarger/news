The Civic Calendar

Every public meeting in Dogtown · Agendas before, recaps after

Four public bodies decide most of what changes in North Little Rock — the City Council, the School Board, the Parks & Recreation Commission, and the Library Board. Their meetings are open to everyone, but the agendas are scattered across four websites and the minutes land weeks later. This page gathers it all: when each body meets, what's on the docket, and what actually happened.

The four bodies

City Council

Second and fourth Mondays, 6:00 PM, City Hall Council Chambers, 300 Main St. Budgets, ordinances, zoning, contracts — the big one.

School Board

NLRSD Board of Education: workshops first Thursdays, regular meetings third Thursdays, 5:30 PM, Administration Building, 2400 Willow St.

Parks & Rec Commission

Third Mondays, 5:00 PM, North Little Rock Community Center, 2700 Willow St. Parks, pools, trails, Burns Park, and community centers.

Library Board

North Little Rock Public Library System Board of Trustees — meets every other month (odd months) at Laman Library.

How we cover it

Before each meeting we post the agenda in plain English — what's up for a vote and why it matters. After, a recap: what passed, what stalled, who said what, sourced to the official record and local coverage. See something we missed? Send a tip — no name required.
